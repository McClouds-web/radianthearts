What we offer

Our Classes

We take children in four small classes. Every class is capped at 18 children, each with its own dedicated teacher.

Baby Class

Warm, one-to-one care. We give full attention to every first step and first word, with gentle guidance and plenty to touch, hear and explore.

Kinder

Freedom within structure. Your toddler chooses, explores and speaks. We build language and confidence through planned activities and outdoor play.

Middle

Curiosity with structure. Early phonics, numbers and group learning, with plenty of room to play and make friends.

Reception

Getting ready for school. Early reading, numbers and life skills woven into play, so children start big school confident and curious.

Joining us

How to Register

1

Book a tour

Call or message us and pick a weekday. Tours take about 30 minutes.

2

Bring your documents

If you bring everything on the day, most families finish registration in one visit.

3

Meet the team

Tell us about your child: routines, allergies and what settles them, so we can prepare properly.

4

Confirm the place

Once the registration fee is settled, we hold the place and confirm in writing.

Good to know

  • Registering does not by itself reserve a place. It is confirmed once fees are settled and we confirm in writing.
  • Places are limited to 18 children per class, so early registration is encouraged.
