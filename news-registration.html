Registration

How Registration Works

Everything you need to know about securing a place for your child.

We accept registrations all year round at our Mogoditshane campus. Because we cap every class at 18 children, places are limited and we allocate them in the order registrations are completed.

Who we can take

We accept children from 9 months to 5 years across four classes:

Baby Class

Kinder

Middle

Reception

How registration works

  1. 1

    Book a tour

    Call or message us and pick a weekday that suits you. Tours take about 30 minutes.

  2. 2

    Bring your documents

    If you bring everything on the day, most families finish registration in that single visit.

  3. 3

    Meet the team

    Tell us about your child: routines, allergies, what settles them, so we can prepare properly.

  4. 4

    Confirm the place

    Once the registration fee is settled, your child's place is held and we confirm in writing.

What it costs

  • Registration fee (one-time)P200
  • Term (three months)P4,500
  • One monthP1,700
  • Daily rateP150

The same tuition applies to every class and schedule. Ask us for the current fee sheet.

Good to know

  • • Registering does not automatically reserve a place. It is confirmed once fees are settled and we confirm in writing.
  • • You can change your days later with 30 days notice.
  • • Full day, morning, afternoon and part-week options are all available.

Ready to register?

Book a tour and we will take you through the whole process in one visit.
