Payment terms
Terms
  • Notice period. One calendar month's written notice is required to withdraw your child or to reduce days, otherwise the deposit is forfeited.
  • Changing your days. Schedule changes need 30 days notice and are subject to availability.
  • Absence. Fees remain payable during illness, holidays and family absence, as your child's place is held.
  • Public holidays. Fees are not reduced for public holidays or school closure days.
  • Late payment. Please speak to us early if you expect a delay so we can agree an arrangement.
  • Fee reviews. Fees are reviewed annually. Parents are given written notice before any change takes effect.

Securing a place

Completing an enrolment form does not by itself reserve a place. A place is confirmed once the registration fee and deposit have been received and the school has confirmed in writing. Classes are limited to 12 children.

Questions about fees?

Call +267 395 1404 or email admissions@radianthearts.co.bw. We are happy to talk through options and find a schedule that works for your family.
