REGISTRATION

How Registration Works

We accept registrations all year round. Places are limited to 18 children per class, so it pays to register early. Book a tour to see the classrooms, meet the team, and complete your registration in a single visit.

Start Registration →
BEFORE YOU REGISTER

What You Will Need

To register your child, please bring a copy of their birth certificate, an up-to-date immunisation card, any medical or allergy notes, and two emergency contacts. Registration is P200. We will walk you through each step.

OUR CAMPUS

Inside Our Campus

Our Mogoditshane campus has bright classrooms, safe shaded garden play areas, and a kitchen where meals are cooked fresh each day. Come and see it for yourself, and meet the team who will look after your child.
