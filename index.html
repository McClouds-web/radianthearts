Kinder

Room to explore. Your child chooses what to do, finds their voice, and grows in confidence through play, friends and time outdoors.

Why Radiant Hearts Exists

Radiant Hearts was started because every child deserves a place where they are known by name. Each child is different, with their own gifts and their own way of learning. We teach the way young children learn best, so your child grows up confident, curious and kind.

Our Vision

To create a world where every child is seen as a unique light, growing with confidence, creativity, and compassion. We imagine schools where learning feels like play, where curiosity is celebrated, and where every child knows they belong.

Our Mission

To provide loving, intentional early learning that helps children discover who they are and what they can become. We combine proven teaching methods with warm relationships to build strong foundations for lifelong learning and growth.

Got Questions?
We Have Answers.

Choosing preschool is one of the most important decisions you'll make. We've answered hundreds of parent questions, and every answer reflects our commitment to transparency, safety, and excellence.

What are your school hours?

Our core program runs from 07:30 to 12:30. We also offer extended holiday care and after-care services until 17:30 for working parents.

How do you handle dietary needs?

We provide nutritious, balanced meals prepared daily. We are fully equipped to manage allergies and specific dietary requirements with a personalized meal plan.

Is there a school uniform?

Yes. It is comfortable and easy to play in, it helps children feel part of the group, and it makes mornings simpler.
